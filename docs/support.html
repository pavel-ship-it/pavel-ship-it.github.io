What to include in your message

  • The app: Fridge Pick, Jewelers Handbook or Lightwhirl, and the app version if you know it.
  • Your device: for example iPhone 15 or iPad Air.
  • System version: iOS or iPadOS (or Android for Fridge Pick), from Settings → General → About; for Lightwhirl, the macOS version from Apple menu → About This Mac.
  • Steps: what you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can. It helps a lot.

Common questions

How do I use Fridge Pick?

The Fridge Pick user manual explains My fridge, filters, I’m hungry, the week plan, the shopping list, allergies and kitchen settings. More answers are on the Fridge Pick support page.

How do I use Jewelers Handbook?

The Jewelers Handbook user manual covers the chain calculators, actual size and 3D, stones, cuts, settings, clasps, carat, ring size and metal weight. More answers are on the Jewelers Handbook support page.

What data do the apps collect?

None. There are no accounts, ads or tracking, and what you enter stays on your device. Details are in each policy: Fridge Pick Privacy Policy, Jewelers Handbook Privacy Policy. All policies are also listed on the Privacy page.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account is needed for any of the apps, and they all work offline. You only need internet to install and update them from the store.

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Use the buttons above or write to dreamcomenull@gmail.com. Please include the details listed in What to include.
